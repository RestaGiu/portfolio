# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, chosen explicitly in the brief as the preferred modern, maintainable stack. The site must remain lightweight and avoid unnecessary dependencies.

## Users

Primary users are European recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Resta Giuliano for senior Product Design roles. They need to understand his positioning, experience, evidence of impact, selected work and contact options within seconds.

## Product Purpose

Present Resta Giuliano as a senior Product Designer specializing in complex B2B SaaS, PropTech, Product Discovery, UX Strategy, Data and AI. Success means moving visitors quickly from a compact professional introduction to substantial case studies and then to contact.

## Positioning

“I design complex products and make them simpler.” The portfolio demonstrates how product, business, behavioral evidence, data and technology are synthesized into clearer systems and measurable outcomes.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is primarily evaluated on desktop and mobile during European hiring processes. The homepage is the main experience and contains the essential professional summary, five case studies and contact information. Real project imagery will be added later through prepared project folders and a reusable carousel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Compact sticky header with About, Projects, LinkedIn and Contact navigation.
- Compact hero with portrait placeholder, positioning, experience, European work context and contact actions.
- Compact About summary followed immediately by the project index and five substantial homepage case studies.
- Each case uses reusable structured content for Problem, Discovery and Solution, metrics, tags and images.
- Accessible carousel with buttons, keyboard support, swipe, stable aspect ratio, captions, placeholder state and lightbox.
- Responsive, accessible, SEO-friendly and easy to extend with future projects or a future Portuguese content layer.
- No automatic translation, autoplay, stock photography, invented project imagery or unsupported claims.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Resta Giuliano. Role: Product Designer. Voice: clear, senior, evidence-led and concise. The experience must be contemporary, editorial, minimal and premium, with strong typography, white space, a disciplined grid, restrained borders and subtle interaction. It must not resemble a generic startup landing page or an overly artistic portfolio.

## Evidence on Hand

The brief provides professional biography, competencies, tools, languages, EU work authorization context, five detailed case studies and the following impact figures: 5+ years, up to +65% completion/conversion, approximately +40% potential revenue, approximately 30% of a customer journey redesigned and -53% complaint tickets year over year. No portrait, screenshots, final email address or LinkedIn URL were supplied; the implementation must expose clearly labeled replacement points and must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Move from identity to evidence quickly.
- Make complex information easy to scan without flattening the reasoning.
- Let project outcomes and decisions carry the visual hierarchy.
- Keep interaction purposeful, restrained and accessible.
- Treat content as structured data so the portfolio can grow without rebuilding its components.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, readable mobile type, reduced motion, touch-friendly controls, semantic structure and no horizontal overflow.
